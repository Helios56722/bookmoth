# Bookmoth brand figure

Bookmoth is an open-source learning workspace that turns screenshots and notes into useful study material. The brand figure should feel curious, capable, calm, and memorable. It must work as an app icon, a small website mark, and a larger mascot without becoming childish or visually crowded.

## Core visual idea

- A moth whose lower wings form the silhouette of an open book.
- Deep ink-violet as the anchor color, with warm amber as the learning spark.
- A clear silhouette that remains readable at 32 pixels.
- Rounded geometry and generous negative space.
- No rendered words or letterforms. The Bookmoth wordmark will be typeset separately.

## Concept set

1. **Open-book moth** — compact, symmetrical emblem for the main app icon.
2. **Lantern scholar** — a capable moth librarian mascot for onboarding and empty states.
3. **Field-guide print** — tactile linocut or risograph figure for editorial graphics.
4. **Orbit mark** — a modern moth and book symbol with a small amber learning light.

## Selection checks

- Recognizable as both a moth and a book without explanation.
- Clear at small sizes with no cramped internal details.
- Original silhouette with no resemblance to an existing mascot or logo.
- No generated text, watermark, or faux lettering.
- Works on light and dark backgrounds.
- Can be simplified into one color later.

The four concepts are generated locally with ComfyUI and Flux. They are visual exploration, not a final trademark clearance or production vector. The selected direction should be redrawn as SVG before release.
